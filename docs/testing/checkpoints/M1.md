## Main success and failure/degradation paths

- Success: Windows GSMTC data/capability reads, six-format decode/tag inspection, Password Vault canary cycle, exact HKCU enable/status/disable, WebView2 discovery and idempotent probe reset all worked on their recorded environments.
- Critical negative path: a corrupt audio fixture was rejected without blocking the other six formats; a Credential read failure fake performed cleanup; missing confirmation flags rejected all mutation commands; reset preserved sibling/unknown files and refused a relative scope.
- Safe fallback: absent Apple capability remains hidden, unavailable AI is irrelevant to M1, autostart/notification remain disabled, and M2 will begin from a local silent shell.
